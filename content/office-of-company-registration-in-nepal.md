## What Company Registration Means in Nepal

Company registration in Nepal refers to the formal process of incorporating a business entity under the prevailing laws of Nepal. The procedure provides the company with a legal identity that allows it to conduct business activities within Nepal's jurisdiction. The Office of the Company Registrar (OCR), operating under the Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, handles this process. It is governed primarily by the Companies Act, 2063 (2006).

Registering a company is a legal obligation for individuals or groups wishing to carry out commercial activities. Once registered, the entity receives a certificate of incorporation which confirms its legal status. This allows the company to open bank accounts, enter into contracts, hire employees, and undertake other business activities as a recognized legal person.

Entities that can register include:
- Private Limited Companies
- Public Limited Companies
- Non-Profit Companies
- Foreign Companies
- Partnership Firms (under separate laws)

The registration process ensures transparency, accountability, and the protection of stakeholders such as shareholders, employees, and customers. Once registered, the company is required to comply with several statutory obligations such as filing annual returns and maintaining proper accounting records.

## Where to Register Your New Company

All company registrations in Nepal must be done at the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR), which is located in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. Regional offices have not yet been assigned this function, so all applicants—regardless of location—must submit their applications to the central office. However, much of the process can now be completed online through the OCR's official website: www.ocr.gov.np.

Registration applications, document submissions, and tracking can be conducted online, reducing the need for frequent physical visits. Final document verification and certificate issuance, however, may still require physical presence or coordination with the OCR.

The online portal also provides facilities such as:
- Name reservation services
- Company document submission
- Status checking

The portal is integrated with other government departments, including the Inland Revenue Department for PAN registration, enhancing the efficiency of the registration process.

## How to Select a Legal Company Name

Choosing a legal company name is the first mandatory step in registering a company in Nepal. The name must be unique, appropriate, and comply with the naming conventions prescribed in the Companies Act, 2063. The proposed name should not resemble any existing company, be misleading, or contain prohibited terms.

Key rules for name selection include:
- Must not be identical or deceptively similar to an existing registered name
- Should not infringe on any trademarks
- Cannot include words such as "government," "national," or offensive terms unless justified
- Must end with "Pvt. Ltd." for private companies and "Ltd." for public companies

The name must be reserved via the OCR online system before initiating the full registration process. Once approved, the name is reserved for 35 days. If registration is not completed within this period, the reservation will lapse.

Supporting laws:
- Companies Act, 2063, Section 9
- Industrial Enterprises Act, 2076 (for sector-specific naming restrictions)

OCR staff may reject the name proposal if it violates any of the naming rules. Applicants must ensure their proposed name does not lead to legal or procedural delays.

## What Types of Companies Can Register

Under the Companies Act, 2063, several types of companies can be registered in Nepal. Each type has its own legal requirements, structure, and obligations:

1. Private Limited Company:
- Minimum of one and maximum of 101 shareholders
- No invitation to the public for share subscription
- Commonly used for small to medium businesses

2. Public Limited Company:
- Minimum of seven shareholders
- Can offer shares to the public
- Requires more compliance and reporting

3. Non-Profit Company:
- Operates under Section 166 of the Companies Act
- No distribution of profits allowed
- Suitable for charitable, educational, and religious organizations

4. Foreign Company:
- Registered as a branch or liaison office
- Requires prior approval from concerned ministries

Each company type must submit specific documents, meet minimum capital requirements, and fulfill post-registration obligations. Selecting the correct company type is essential as it affects taxation, governance, and financial reporting.

## How to Prepare Registration Documents

Preparation of documents is a crucial part of the registration process. These documents must be accurate, complete, and in compliance with Nepalese legal standards. Incomplete or incorrectly prepared documents are the primary reasons for application rejections.

Required documents include:
- Application Form (as prescribed by OCR)
- Memorandum of Association (MOA)
- Articles of Association (AOA)
- Copy of citizenship or passport of shareholders and directors
- Agreement among promoters (if applicable)
- Details of company office address
- Passport-size photos of directors
- Power of Attorney (if submitted by an agent)

All documents must be signed and notarized. Foreign promoters must legalize documents through their respective embassies or consulates. OCR may require additional documents depending on the nature of the business.

Legal references:
- Companies Act, 2063, Section 4
- OCR Guidelines for Document Submission

## What Is the Process of Registration

The registration process consists of sequential steps to be followed by the applicant. The process must be strictly adhered to in order to ensure approval without delay.

Step-by-Step Process:
1. Reserve the company name on the OCR portal
2. Prepare the required documents as per OCR checklist
3. Submit the online application with all documents
4. Pay the registration fee through the bank voucher or online gateway
5. Wait for verification of documents by OCR staff
6. Receive a notice for corrections if any discrepancies are found
7. Resubmit corrected documents (if required)
8. Receive Certificate of Incorporation upon approval
9. Proceed to PAN/VAT registration through IRD

The entire process usually takes between 7–15 working days if documents are accurate and complete.

## What Are the Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility for company registration is primarily based on legal capacity and compliance with the Companies Act. Both individuals and entities can be promoters, provided they meet the prescribed criteria:

Individual Eligibility:
- Must be at least 18 years of age
- Must not be declared mentally incapacitated
- Should not be blacklisted by any financial institution
- Must possess legal citizenship or passport

Company Eligibility:
- Registered entities can act as promoters
- Must provide authorization through Board Resolutions

Additional requirements for foreign nationals include:
- Certified passport copy
- Proof of address and legal entry

Legal references:
- Companies Act, 2063, Section 3

Failure to meet eligibility requirements will result in rejection at the document verification stage.

## How Long the Registration Process Takes

The typical timeline for completing company registration in Nepal ranges from 7 to 15 working days. Several factors influence this timeframe:
- Accuracy of documents submitted
- Workload at OCR during the submission
- Type of company being registered
- Need for name re-submission or document clarification

Online name reservation usually takes 1–2 days. Document review and certificate issuance take an additional 5–10 days. If resubmissions are required, the process can extend beyond 15 days.

## What Is the Cost to Register Company

The total cost of registration depends on the authorized capital of the proposed company. The higher the capital, the higher the registration fee.

Fee Structure (Private Company):
- Up to NPR 100,000: NPR 1,000
- NPR 100,001–500,000: NPR 4,500
- NPR 500,001–2,500,000: NPR 9,500
- NPR 2,500,001–10,000,000: NPR 16,000
- Above NPR 10,000,000: NPR 19,000

Other costs:
- Legal/consultancy fees (if applicable)
- Notary public charges
- Document preparation costs

Official fees are fixed by OCR and are non-refundable.

## What Are the Government Fees Payable

Government fees are determined based on the company's proposed capital. These include:
- Registration fee (as detailed above)
- Stamp duty for notarization
- PAN application processing fee
- Renewal fee (applicable yearly)

These fees are payable to the Office of Company Registrar and Inland Revenue Department. Failure to pay correct fees results in rejection of the application.

## What Laws Govern the Registration Process

The registration and operation of companies in Nepal are governed by multiple statutes. The key legal documents include:
- Companies Act, 2063: Main legal framework
- Industrial Enterprises Act, 2076: Sector-specific requirements
- Income Tax Act, 2058: Tax compliance requirements
- Foreign Investment and Technology Transfer Act, 2075: For foreign entities
- Labor Act, 2074: Post-registration HR compliance

Applicants must adhere to the relevant laws during and after registration.

## What Authority Handles Registrations in Nepal

The Office of the Company Registrar (OCR) is the competent authority. It operates under the Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies. It is responsible for:
- Accepting and reviewing company registration applications
- Approving or rejecting company names
- Issuing Certificates of Incorporation
- Monitoring compliance and maintaining company records

Other involved authorities include:
- Inland Revenue Department (for PAN)
- Local government bodies (for trade license)
- Department of Industry (for foreign companies)

## How to Ensure All Requirements Are Met

Meeting all legal and procedural requirements is essential. Applicants should:
- Use the OCR-provided checklist
- Double-check document accuracy
- Ensure all documents are notarized
- Follow the correct name reservation steps
- Submit fees using authorized payment methods

Using legal advisors or registration consultants can improve compliance and reduce rejection risks.

## What Services Are Needed Post Registration

After registration, a company must comply with several post-incorporation obligations:
- Obtain PAN and VAT registration
- Open a corporate bank account
- Appoint auditors and file initial audit report
- Issue share certificates
- Register with local ward office (if applicable)
- Comply with tax and labor laws

Non-compliance can result in fines, blacklisting, or cancellation of the company registration.

## What Common Errors You Must Avoid

Avoiding errors during registration ensures faster approval:
- Submitting incomplete or incorrect documents
- Proposing non-compliant company names
- Ignoring OCR guidelines
- Missing mandatory notarization
- Providing inconsistent personal details

Consulting experienced professionals can reduce the chance of errors.

## FAQs

### How many days for company registration?
It usually takes between 7 to 15 working days to complete company registration in Nepal. The duration depends on the accuracy of submitted documents and OCR's internal workload. Errors or omissions may cause delays.

### Can a foreigner register a company?
Yes, foreigners can register a company in Nepal subject to compliance with the Foreign Investment and Technology Transfer Act, 2075. Proper approvals from the Department of Industry and related ministries are required.

### Is online registration available now?
Yes, online registration is available through the OCR portal. The entire application process, including name reservation and document submission, can be completed digitally.

### What is the official registration fee?
The registration fee ranges from NPR 1,000 to NPR 19,000, depending on the authorized capital of the proposed company. Fees must be paid at the time of application.

### What documents are needed initially?
Key documents include the application form, MOA, AOA, citizenship/passport of promoters, office address, and photographs. All documents must be notarized before submission.

### Is PAN registration included automatically?
No, PAN registration must be applied separately through the Inland Revenue Department after receiving the Certificate of Incorporation from OCR.

### Do I need an office address?
Yes, a valid business address is mandatory. It must be documented with proof and used consistently in all registration forms.

### What is the minimum capital required?
There is no fixed minimum capital requirement by law. However, practical thresholds apply based on the nature of the business and OCR fee slabs.

### Is a company name check mandatory?
Yes, name reservation is mandatory. The proposed name must be approved and reserved before proceeding with document submission.

### Can I register without legal help?
Yes, individuals can register without legal assistance. However, using legal services ensures compliance, reduces rejection risk, and improves application quality.
