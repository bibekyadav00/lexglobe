## What Is the Company Registrar Office

The Company Registrar Office (CRO) in Nepal is a government agency under the Ministry of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies. It is responsible for handling all legal aspects of company registration, regulation, and monitoring in Nepal. The CRO maintains an official database of all registered companies within the country. This includes private limited companies, public limited companies, non-profit organizations, and foreign company branches.

The CRO ensures that all registered entities comply with the Companies Act, 2063 (2006), and other applicable regulations. It provides services such as company incorporation, name approval, legal compliance filing, and public access to company records. Individuals can access CRO services either by visiting the physical office in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu, or through the official online portal.

## How the Company Database Is Maintained

The CRO maintains a centralized electronic database of all company records. This includes name registration details, incorporation status, legal representatives, capital structure, shareholding pattern, and compliance status. The database is updated in real-time as new applications are processed and existing companies update their legal or financial status.

All data is managed under the provisions of the Companies Act, 2063 and the Electronic Transactions Act, 2063. The online portal is integrated with security protocols to ensure data accuracy and public access without compromising sensitive information. This system helps users verify company status, check if a name is already registered, and view relevant filings.

## Where to Access the Company Name Records

Company name records in Nepal can be accessed through:
- Official Website: www.ocr.gov.np
- Department of Industry: For additional records related to foreign companies or industrial entities
- Public Notice Boards at CRO: For latest notices regarding registration or legal compliance

The CRO's online system allows real-time search functionality, ensuring fast and accurate verification. Users do not need to log in or create an account to perform basic name searches.

## How to Search for a Company Name Online

To check if a company name is already registered, follow these steps:
1. Visit the official CRO portal: www.ocr.gov.np
2. Click on "Search Registered Companies"
3. Enter the proposed or full company name in the search field
4. Click on the search icon
5. Review the results for matching entries

The system will display:
- Company name
- Registration number
- Registration date
-Company status (active, dissolved, etc.)

Users can conduct multiple searches without any restrictions.

## What Are the Eligibility Conditions to Search

There are no legal eligibility restrictions on who can check for a registered company name in Nepal. Any individual can search:
- Existing or potential business owners
- Legal practitioners and consultants
- Public users conducting due diligence
- Students and researchers

Name searches are a public service provided for transparency under Section 3 of the Companies Act, 2063. There is no requirement to be affiliated with the business to perform a search.

## What Documents Are Needed to Search Names

For a basic name search, no documents are required. However, for formal verification or name reservation, the following documents may be needed:
- Citizenship certificate or passport (for individual applicants)
- Board resolution (for corporate applicants)
- Application form (available online)
- Proposed business objectives (optional but recommended)

These documents are not required for casual checking but are mandatory during the name reservation or registration stages.

## What Are the Steps to Search a Name

Here is a clear process for searching a company name:
- Step 1: Visit www.ocr.gov.np
- Step 2: Click "Search Registered Companies"
- Step 3: Enter full or partial company name
- Step 4: Press the search button
- Step 5: Review the results carefully

If no match is found, the name may be available for registration. If a match appears, check status and details.

## How Long the Search Process Takes

The online name search process usually takes less than 5 minutes. Since the CRO portal operates on a real-time database, users get immediate results upon entering the name.

For offline or formal verification (e.g., requesting a certified name verification letter), it can take 1 to 2 working days depending on the volume of requests and the type of verification required.

There is no waiting period for basic searches done through the website.

## What Is the Cost for Name Search

- Online Search: Free of charge for all users
- Certified Copy/Letter of Confirmation: NPR 100 to NPR 500 depending on urgency and format

These charges are outlined in the schedule of fees published by the Office of the Company Registrar. All charges must be paid through official banking channels.

## What Are the Official Government Charges

The standard fees, if you want an official document or letter, are:
- NPR 100 for general confirmation
- NPR 250 for urgent processing (within 24 hours)
- NPR 500 for certified legal verification letter

These fees are governed by the Office of the Company Registrar (Regulation of Fees) Rule, 2073.

## What Is the Legal Basis for Name Search

The legal right to access company name information is provided under:
- Companies Act, 2063 (Section 3 & 4)
- Electronic Transactions Act, 2063
- Right to Information Act, 2064

These laws ensure transparency and public access to business registration data. The Registrar is legally required to maintain and make available the company database.

## Which Authority Handles the Name Database

The Office of the Company Registrar (CRO) under the Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies is responsible for:
- Maintaining the name database
- Providing name availability status
- Issuing name verification certificates
- Updating name changes and legal status

The CRO operates the www.ocr.gov.np portal and ensures compliance with national laws.

## How to Confirm Legal Status of Company

To confirm whether a company is legally registered and active:
- Search the company name on www.ocr.gov.np
- Check for status: "Active," "Closed," or "Dissolved"
- Note registration number and date
- Confirm whether the directors and objectives are as per public record

This confirmation can be used for legal and business transactions. If a certified copy is required, submit an application to CRO.

## What Is the Checklist for Name Verification

- Company name (full or partial)
- Spelling accuracy
- Language consistency (Nepali or English)
- Legal suffix (e.g., Pvt. Ltd.)
- Similar or conflicting names
- Online search result screenshot
- Application if reserving the name
- ID documents if applying officially

This checklist ensures that a name is properly checked before proposing for registration.

## How to Request Official Name Confirmation

To request an official confirmation letter:
- Fill out the Name Verification Application Form
- Attach applicant's ID or business authorization
- Submit to the CRO in person or via online portal
- Pay the applicable government fee

The CRO will issue a formal letter confirming the status of the name and whether it is available, reserved, or already registered. This document is useful for banks, legal filings, or cross-border verification.

## FAQs

### How do I search company name?
Visit www.ocr.gov.np, click on "Search Registered Companies," enter the name and review the listed companies. It is free and open to the public.

### Is there an online search system?
Yes, the Office of the Company Registrar has an official online portal that provides real-time search features for anyone to check registered company names in Nepal.

### What website shows registered names?
The official website to check company names is www.ocr.gov.np. It is maintained by the Government of Nepal.

### Can anyone check my company name?
Yes, any member of the public can check company name status. This ensures transparency and follows the Companies Act.

### Is it free to check names?
Yes, online searches are free. Fees only apply if you need a certified confirmation or verification letter.

### Do I need my company ID?
No, for basic searches you only need the name. But for official requests or applications, ID documents are required.

### What if name is already taken?
You cannot register a name that is already taken. Consider a variation or a different suffix to ensure uniqueness.

### Can I get official confirmation?
Yes, submit an application to the CRO along with your ID and pay a nominal fee. You will receive a certified confirmation letter.

### How to check registration status?
Use the same online search tool and review the "status" field beside each company name to confirm if it's active or closed.

### Which authority manages company names?
The Office of the Company Registrar (CRO), operating under the Ministry of Industry, manages the official company name database in Nepal.
