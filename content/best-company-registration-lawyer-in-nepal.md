## What Is Company Registration in Nepal

Company registration in Nepal is a legal procedure that allows individuals or groups to operate a recognized business entity under the laws of Nepal. The process is governed by the Companies Act, 2006 and is regulated by the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR). Registration enables a business to obtain legal recognition, tax identification, and the right to enter into contracts.

Types of companies that can be registered in Nepal:
- Private Limited Company
- Public Limited Company
- Non-Profit Company (Not Distributing Profit)

Registering a company includes name reservation, submission of documents, verification, and obtaining a certificate of incorporation.

## What Are the Legal Requirements Today

To register a company in Nepal, legal conditions as per the Companies Act, 2006 include:
- Minimum of one director and one shareholder (for private companies)
- Company name must be unique and not already registered
- Office address in Nepal
- Submission of required statutory documents
- Objective of the business must be lawful

These requirements are assessed during the application and approval stages by the OCR.

## Where to Register a New Company

All company registrations are filed and approved by the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR) located in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. Applications can also be submitted online via ocr.gov.np.

Types of businesses to register here include:
- Sole Proprietorships (via local ward offices)
- Partnerships (via respective offices)
- Private and Public Limited Companies (via OCR)

## What Documents Are Needed for Registration

Required documents for company registration in Nepal include:
- Application in prescribed format (as per Schedule 1 of Companies Act)
- Proposed Memorandum of Association (MOA)
- Proposed Articles of Association (AOA)
- Copy of citizenship certificates of promoters
- Signed copy of agreement between promoters
- Passport-sized photos of promoters

All documents must be submitted either physically at OCR or digitally through its official portal.

## How to File Company Application Forms

The application process includes:
1. Reserve company name through OCR portal
2. Fill online registration form with details of business
3. Upload scanned copies of MOA, AOA, citizenship, agreement
4. Pay registration fee as per authorized capital
5. Submit application online or in person

Following submission, the OCR examines documents and issues a certificate if everything complies.

## What Are Government Charges and Fees

As per the Companies Act, government fees are calculated based on the authorized capital:
- Up to NPR 1,00,000 – NPR 1,000
- NPR 1,00,001 to 5,00,000 – NPR 4,500
- NPR 5,00,001 to 25,00,000 – NPR 9,500
- NPR 25,00,001 to 1,00,00,000 – NPR 16,000
- Above NPR 1,00,00,000 – Additional charges apply

These fees are payable directly to the OCR.

## What Laws Apply to Company Formation

The main law governing company formation in Nepal is the Companies Act, 2006. Other relevant legal frameworks include:
- Industrial Enterprises Act, 2020
- Income Tax Act, 2058
- Value Added Tax Act, 2052
- Foreign Investment and Technology Transfer Act (if FDI involved)

Compliance with these laws is mandatory for new companies.

## Which Authorities Handle Registrations

The following authorities are involved in company registration:
- Office of the Company Registrar (OCR) – Main registration body
- Inland Revenue Department (IRD) – PAN and VAT registration
- Local Ward Offices – Business registration at local level
- Department of Industry – For manufacturing and FDI companies

All entities must register with OCR first before approaching other offices.
